# What's new

## 1.1.0

- **Network** page (replaces Devices): a map of your whole network, from the Internet and your router down to every switch, access point and device, plus your Tailscale machines. Switch to a list any time.
- Click a device to see its address, maker, how it's connected, ping, and the services it runs (web pages open with one click).
- NetPull works out what new devices are by trying a few common ports, and picks an icon to match.
- **Optional UniFi connection** (read-only): data used and live speed for every device, Wi-Fi signal and band, which switch port or access point it's on, and what it's doing (streaming, gaming, updates...).
- **All devices** on the Graph shows your whole network's traffic, with the busiest devices at any point.
- **Devices** tab in Usage, with CSV export.
- New alerts: a device goes offline or comes back (switch it on per device), a device uses a lot of data, and a monthly data limit per device.

## 1.0.0

The first version of NetPull.

- **Graph**: live download and upload graph, plus 3 hours, 24 hours, a week and a month of history. Click a point to see which apps were busy.
- **Usage**: totals for each app and each host, for today, yesterday, this week, this month, 30 days and 90 days.
- **Connections**: every open connection with host name, country, ISP, port, state and live speed. Search, sort, copy and look up.
- **Devices**: quiet checks of your home network every 5 minutes, with names and a trusted switch.
- **Alerts**: new app online, app changed, heavy data use, data cap and new device.
- Adapter filter, mini floating graph, live tray icon, CSV export and automatic updates.
