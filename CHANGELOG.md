# What's new

## 1.0.0

The first version of NetPull.

- **Graph**: live download and upload graph, plus 3 hours, 24 hours, a week and a month of history. Click a point to see which apps were busy.
- **Usage**: totals for each app and each host, for today, yesterday, this week, this month, 30 days and 90 days.
- **Connections**: every open connection with host name, country, ISP, port, state and live speed. Search, sort, copy and look up.
- **Devices**: quiet checks of your home network every 5 minutes, with names and a trusted switch.
- **Alerts**: new app online, app changed, heavy data use, data cap and new device.
- Adapter filter, mini floating graph, live tray icon, CSV export and automatic updates.
