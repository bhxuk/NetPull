Download and run the installer

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't code-signed.

Say yes to one admin prompt

Windows only lets administrators see which app owns each byte. You're asked once, at install.

Leave it in the tray

NetPull starts when you sign in and keeps recording. The tray icon is a tiny live graph.

It watches your network. Nobody watches it.

Your history stays on your PC

A small database in your own app data folder. No account, nothing uploaded, delete it any time from Settings.

You choose the lookups

Countries and ISPs come from ip-api.com, device makers from macvendors.com. Only addresses are sent, and both switch off.

It only watches

No driver, no firewall changes, nothing blocked. It reads the network events Windows already records.

Questions

Does it slow my PC down?

No. It adds traffic up once a second in memory and writes to its history once a minute.

Why do totals look higher with all adapters?

VPNs like Tailscale wrap your traffic, so it's counted once on the VPN adapter and again on Ethernet or Wi-Fi. Pick one adapter at the top of a page to see just that one.

How long is history kept?

90 days by default, or 30 days or a year. Minute-by-minute detail is kept for a week, hourly totals for the rest.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Usage and Connections export to CSV, and Settings exports every day's usage per app.

Does it update itself?

Yes, from the GitHub releases page. You can turn that off in Settings.
